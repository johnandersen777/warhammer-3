# Total War: Warhammer 3: Grand Cathay Guide (Tips, Units, & Legendary Lord)

By [Jack McNulty](/author/jack-mcnulty/)

Published Mar 12, 2022, 11:00 AM EST

Grand Cathay is one of the totally new factions introduced in **_[Total War: Warhammer III](https://screenrant.com/tag/total-war/)_** and offers exciting new units with a strong defensive playstyle. Heavily influenced by east-Asian mythology, Grand Cathay is a kingdom ruled by dragons who take on human form. Before the game's events, Shen-Zoo, the younger sister of one of the legendary lords, Miao Ying, disappeared on a journey beyond the Norscan mountains. Miao Ying and Zhao Ming seek to find the dying bear-god of Kislev, Ursun, and request information on the whereabouts of Shen-Zoo.

Unlike other factions represented in the _Total War: Warhammer_ series, Cathay is not a faction from the original _Warhammer Fantasy Battles_ tabletop wargame. While the faction existed in lore, it did not have units or an army. [_Total War: Warhammer III_ is the first time](https://screenrant.com/total-war-warhammer-grand-cathay-trailer/) players of either the _Warhammer_ tabletop or computer games will be able to see and play with Grand Cathay.

Harmony is the trademark ability of Grand Cathay. In both battle and on the campaign map, the balance of Yin and Yang can provide great bonuses to players who maintain it. Characters, buildings, technology, and certain events on the campaign map can provide Yin or Yang points. The rewards are faction wide and include buffs to diplomatic relations, construction discounts, growth, income, control, corruption resistance, and an ability called Ancestral Warriors, which allows all armies to summon a special unit once per battle. On the battlefield, melee units are considered Yang and ranged units are considered Yin. When Yin and Yang's units are near each other, Yang units will gain bonuses to leadership and [melee defence in _Total War: Warhammer III_](https://screenrant.com/total-war-warhammer-3-unit-stats-guide/), while Yin units have leadership and reload skill buffed. These buffs can, in turn, be buffed by certain units and characters with the Harmony Amplification ability.

## Total War: Warhammer 3: Tips for Grand Cathay in Battle

With a few exceptions, the roster of units available to Grand Cathay is designed around a defensive playstyle. The player has several incentives to adopt a stationary formation and use ranged units to draw the enemy into an attack. The Jade Warrior unit variants, which will form the core of most armies, have the Defensive Stance ability, which grants armor and charge resistance bonuses as long as the unit stays still. Battle Harmony, which requires proximity between Yin and Yang units, is also easier to maintain when units remain still.

The Legendary Lords of Cathay, Miao Ying, and Zhao Ming [both have human and dragon forms.](https://screenrant.com/elden-ring-beat-flying-dragon-agheel-guide/) They are potent spellcasters with massive harmony amplification bonuses in human form but have limited melee capabilities with no armor-piercing damage. In dragon form, they gain significant armor buffs, flight, speed, and extra damage resistance at low health and become extremely powerful melee monsters at the price of most of their spellcasting ability. Miao Ying has access to a more potent repertoire of spells, while Zhao Ming has slightly better melee proficiency. As for non-legendary lords, Cathay has access to two wizards, lore of Ying and lore of Yang, and one melee lord. The wizards are exceptionally weak in melee and should be kept off the front line. The melee lord is somewhat lackluster and functions more as a buffing character than a combat character. Cathay's casting is boosted by its two heroes, [both of which are wizards](https://screenrant.com/total-war-warhammer-3-kislev-faction-guide/). The astromancer and the alchemist, who use lore of metal and lore of heavens, respectively, can inflict huge damage and provide buffs in battle.

Cavalry and infantry units come in three tiers for Cathay. These are Peasant, Jade Warriors, and Celestial Dragons. While useful in the early game because of their cheap cost, the peasant units are quickly made obsolete by their lack of armor and armor-piercing damage. Jade warriors will be the front-line components of most armies with their strong armor and defensive stance. Celestial dragons are very expensive, and so except for the late game, high tier armies, they will generally do best as reserves to deal with [heavily armored foes](https://screenrant.com/skyrim-pros-cons-leveling-heavy-armor-skill-tree/). Celestial dragons don't depend on defensive stance for their survivability and are more capable of the attack than jade warriors. A few missile infantry units fall outside this order, including crane gunners, an extremely long-range armor-piercing and shielded missile unit, and iron hail gunners, a short-range armor-piercing missile unit. These units have negligible melee abilities and should be kept at the rear. The same applies to Cathay's artillery units, of which there are two, one armor-piercing and one non-armor-piercing similar to the empire's hellstorm rocket battery. Unlike other factions such as the [Ogre Kingdoms, Cathay has only one monstrous unit](https://screenrant.com/total-war-warhammer-3-best-starts-beginners-guide/) in the form of the terracotta sentinel, which is unbreakable, has high armor-piercing damage and armor, and a massive health pool. Finally, Cathay has two flying missile units, the sky lantern and the sky junk. Both are relatively delicate and cannot land, so their battle is over once they are out of ammunition. The Wu Xing War Compass is Cathay's magic chariot which serves as a platform for buffing magic and can cast a few spells of its own.

The start of Grand Cathay's campaign is fairly tame relative to other factions such as the Demon Prince of Chaos Undivided. Players will have to put down rebels and reunite the remaining Cathay factions through [conquest or diplomacy](https://screenrant.com/diplomacy-not-option-game-preview/). Because of the diplomacy buffs from Harmony, Grand Cathay has an easier time than most convincing lesser factions to confederate. The great bastion, a series of gates, will essentially protect the area of Cathay from chaos waste invasions. Grand Cathay is a strong faction with a fun series of unique units and mechanics for players to take advantage of in their quest to recover the lost sister Shen-Zoo.

_**Total War: Warhammer III**_ is available on PC, Mac, and Linux.
